# Learning with AI

AI assistants can be the best tutor you've ever had, or a shortcut that leaves you unable to code without them. This repo is designed for the first outcome. Here's the philosophy behind it.

## The deal

**You write the code. The AI makes you better at writing it.**

| The AI will | The AI won't |
|---|---|
| Explain concepts as many times and ways as you need | Write your project |
| Explain error messages | Fix your project's bugs for you |
| Give hints, one step at a time | Skip the hint ladder because you're in a hurry |
| Review your code honestly against a rubric | Pass you out of kindness |
| Quiz you and track your progress | Edit the curriculum during your sessions |
| Reply in your language | |

The detailed rules are in [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).

## Why so strict?

Because the goal isn't a finished project, it's **you being able to build the next one**. Every time the AI writes code you should have written, you skip the struggle that creates the skill. Employers, clients and your future self need the skill, not the file.

The understanding check in every review exists for the same reason. If you can't explain your code, the level isn't passed, no matter who wrote it.

## How to get the most out of your mentor

1. **Try first.** Spend 20–30 minutes before asking. Bring your attempt.
2. **Ask specific questions.** Goal, what you tried, what happened, what you expected.
3. **Ask "why", not just "how".** "Why does this need a loop?" teaches more than "how do I loop?"
4. **Explain back.** "So a dictionary is like... is that right?" Teaching is the fastest way to learn.
5. **Use reviews as conversations.** Disagree with a score? Ask the AI to point to the rubric line. Learn from the argument.
6. **Verify.** AI can be wrong. Run the code. Check the docs.

## AI in your future job

Professional developers use AI every day. Later levels teach you to use it as a productive engineer: reviewing AI-generated code, writing specs for agents, and (in Level 09) building AI systems yourself. You can only judge AI output if you understand the fundamentals, and that's what Levels 00–08 give you.
